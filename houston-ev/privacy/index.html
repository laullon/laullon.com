Privacy Policy

HOUSTON EV helps drivers plan electric vehicle trips and charging stops.

Information You Enter

The app stores vehicle profiles, favorite destinations, favorite chargers, unit preferences, trip settings, and active trip state on your device.

Location

HOUSTON EV may use your current location when you choose current-location planning or active-trip recalculation. Location is used to estimate range and plan the next charging leg.

Network Services

HOUSTON EV uses Apple Maps and Open Charge Map to calculate routes and find public charging stations. Route points, destination coordinates, and charger-search coordinates may be sent to those services to provide planning results.

Purchases

HOUSTON EV uses Apple's StoreKit for its annual subscription. Payment information is handled by Apple.

Data We Do Not Sell

HOUSTON EV does not sell personal data.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact german@laullon.com.
