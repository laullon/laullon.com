Driving Safety

HOUSTON EV is an EV planning aid. Always verify charger status and the final route in your navigation app before driving.

Blocked Chargers and OCM Reports

Blocked chargers is a Pro feature. Use Do not use this charger to exclude a station from planning. In Home → Blocked chargers, you can allow it again after confirmation. Blocking stays private. Stored blocks are preserved if Pro expires, but only applied while Pro is active; renewing or restoring Pro reactivates them.

Pro users can choose Report nonexistent charger for entries with a stored OCM record ID. Confirm only when you have verified that the charger does not exist. The app opens that exact OCM listing: choose Edit, sign in, propose the appropriate correction, and submit it for approval. A comment alone does not remove a listing, and no deletion or approval time is guaranteed. The charger stays locally blocked. Entries without an OCM record ID cannot use this shortcut. Read OCM's contributor guidance.

Experimental Energy Planner

The optional experimental energy planner compares estimated driving and charging time with flexible charging targets. It uses generic charging assumptions, so estimates are not guaranteed vehicle-specific charging times or a globally optimal route. The default planner excludes charging duration from total trip time.

Languages

The app interface is available in English and Spanish (Spain). Place names, charger information, map labels, subscription pricing, and text entered by the user may use the language supplied by Apple Maps, Open Charge Map, StoreKit, or the user.
