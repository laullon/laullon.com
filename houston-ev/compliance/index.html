Product Safety and Compliance

Product safety, compliance labels, and contact information for HOUSTON EV.

Product Identification

Product name: HOUSTON EV. Product type: iOS software application for electric vehicle trip planning and charging-stop planning.

Labels and Markings

HOUSTON EV is a software-only mobile application and is not a physical product, hardware device, toy, appliance, radio equipment, or personal protective equipment. It does not include physical product safety labels, CE markings, batch numbers, serial numbers, or packaging labels.

Safety Information

HOUSTON EV is an EV planning aid. Always verify charger status, road conditions, route restrictions, and final navigation instructions in your navigation app before driving. The app does not control your vehicle, provide live vehicle battery integration, guarantee charger availability, or replace driver judgment.
