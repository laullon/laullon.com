HOUSTON EV

Plan EV route legs, charging stops, saved trips, live trip checks, and navigation handoff before the road gets complicated.

Download on the App Store Privacy Policy

Practical planning before you drive

Set up your vehicle, choose a start point and destination, enter battery level and consumption, then compare route options with charging legs calculated by HOUSTON EV.

Vehicle-aware range

Save battery capacity, consumption, unit preferences, and trip settings so each plan starts from the numbers that matter.

Charging leg checks

Estimate safe range, expected arrival battery, and whether a route leg is reachable, risky, or needs a charging stop.

Navigation handoff

Send the next charger first, then the final destination when you are ready to continue in your navigation app.

Favorite places

Keep favorite destinations and trusted chargers close when you plan frequent routes.

Saved trips

Save reusable trip plans so regular routes are ready when you come back.

Public charger lookup

Search public charging stations and filter by minimum charger power for more useful charging options.

During-trip updates

Update battery or consumption during a trip so HOUSTON EV can recalculate the current leg when conditions change.

HOUSTON EV is a planning aid. Always verify charger status and the final route in your navigation app before driving.
