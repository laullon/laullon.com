HOUSTON EV

Plan the journey and the drive back with battery-aware routes, compatible charging stops, and clear range checks.

What’s new in 1.2

New in version 1.2

Plan beyond the first leg

Version 1.2 adds round-trip planning and a fuller nearby-charger search, alongside clearer route comparison and a simpler trip setup.

Round-trip planning

Calculate the return using the battery expected at your destination and see when you should charge before heading back.

Nearby compatible chargers

Search near your location, a favorite destination, or any point on the map using your vehicle and charger preferences.

Cleaner route decisions

Compare practical alternatives with planned charging stops, distance, drive time, and expected arrival battery together.

Practical confidence before you drive

HOUSTON EV uses your vehicle, battery, consumption, reserve, connector, and power requirements to build a plan you can review before leaving.

Battery-aware planning

See safe range, expected arrival battery, and whether each leg is reachable, risky, or needs a charging stop.

Your places and preferences

Keep favorite destinations, trusted chargers, saved trips, and charger-network preferences ready for future plans.

Navigate your way

Send the next stop or route to Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze when you are ready to drive.

Adapt during the trip

Update battery or consumption as conditions change, then recheck the current leg and suggested charging plan.

iPhone and CarPlay

Keep the active trip synchronized while CarPlay presents a concise, driver-focused view of the next target.

English and Spanish

Use the complete app interface in English or Spanish (Spain), including trip planning and CarPlay.

HOUSTON EV is a planning aid and does not provide live charger availability. Always verify charger status and the final route in your navigation app before driving.
